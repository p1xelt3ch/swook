Our story

Built for unhurried conversation.

Ember & Oak started as a weekend pop-up and grew into the corner café our neighborhood needed — a place where the coffee is serious but the vibe is easy.

We source beans from small farms, compost our grounds, and keep the playlist low enough to hear your friend across the table.
